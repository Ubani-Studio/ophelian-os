# Trajectories

The Bóveda trajectory system. Ten primary arc patterns, each
distilled to a single word that brands cleanly. Each primary has
three to five named variants drawn from specific traditions —
African / Asian / Western / diasporic — with attribution.

The primary set is the brand; the variants are the lineage. A
creator picks a primary at the trajectory level (this Cube is a
*Going*) and optionally pins a specific variant for cultural
texture (a *Going* in the Sundiata tradition).

This is a curated decolonial substrate. The Hero's Journey sits as
one named variant among many, not as the universal default.

## The ten primary trajectories

Each carries a glyph for UI marking, mirroring the Subtaste twelve.

### 1. Going · ⤴

The journey out. Threshold-crossed, trials encountered. Most
celebrated arc in fiction, often universalised as the only arc.
Here it sits as one of ten.

Variants:
- Hero's Journey (Campbell, Greek-rooted Western synthesis)
- Sundiata (Mande epic, founding journey of the king-in-exile)
- Mahabharata · Arjuna (Indian, warrior's confrontation)
- Three-Act (Western dramatic structure)
- Send-Off (universal coming-of-age)

### 2. Trick · ⌘

The wit-and-cunning arc. The protagonist outflanks force with
intelligence, language, or laughter. Anchored in trickster
mythologies that cross every continent.

Variants:
- Ananse Cycle (Akan, the spider trickster)
- Èṣù Crossroads (Yoruba, the lord of contingency)
- Br'er Rabbit (African American)
- Pícaro (Spanish, picaresque novel)
- Heist (modern Western)

### 3. Lineage · ⫷

The generational arc. The present moment is the accumulated weight
of ancestors. Time runs vertically as well as horizontally. A
favoured form across cultures with strong ancestral practice.

Variants:
- Ifá Odu Pattern (Yoruba divinatory cosmology, 256 odu)
- Oríkì Praise-Lineage (Yoruba praise poetry)
- Mahabharata Family Epic (Indian, the Kuru lineage)
- Roots (African American, Haley)
- Begats (Hebraic biblical)

### 4. Return · ↺

The homecoming. Exile, distance, then return — but the returning
self is changed. Distinct from *Going* because the arc completes
rather than transforms; the moment is the threshold of arrival, not
of leaving.

Variants:
- Odyssey (Greek)
- Sankofa (Akan, "go back to fetch what was lost")
- Ramayana · Banishment-Return (Indian)
- Prodigal (Hebraic)
- Diasporic Return (modern African and Caribbean homecoming)

### 5. Refusal · ⊘

The arc of the *no* that makes a world. Protagonist refuses the
order they were born into; the refusal itself births something new.
Not rebellion-with-aim; refusal as form.

Variants:
- Maroon Flight (Caribbean / South American escape and self-rule)
- Abolitionist Arc (African American)
- Bartleby (Melville, Western literary)
- Satyagraha (Gandhi, Indian, civil disobedience)
- Anti-Bildungsroman (modern, refusing the come-of-age contract)

### 6. Hold · ▢

Stillness as form. Not absence of action — sustained presence.
Eastern traditions are richest here; the West has slow cinema and
the contemplative novel. Action is incidental; observation is the
event.

Variants:
- Kishōtenketsu (Japanese 4-act, no central conflict)
- Kabuki Stillness (Japanese, mié pose as event)
- Slow Cinema (modern, Tarr / Tsai / Reygadas)
- Contemplative Tale (Buddhist, Zen koan as story)
- Vigil (universal, holding-watch arcs)

### 7. Crossing · ✛

The threshold between worlds. Bridge, river, port, crossroads, the
line between life and death. Diasporic cosmologies live here; the
crossing is the substrate of Black Atlantic experience.

Variants:
- Kalfou (Vodou crossroads, Mèt Kalfou the gatekeeper)
- Charon's Ferry (Greek)
- Òrun-Ayé (Yoruba, sky and earth as crossing)
- Afrofuturist Portal (modern, Octavia Butler / Sun Ra)
- Bridge Narrative (universal)

### 8. Pact · ⊗

The bargain with power. A deal that grants but demands return.
Older than any tradition — the contract with a non-human force.

Variants:
- Faustian Bargain (German / Western literary)
- Blues Crossroads (African American, Robert Johnson)
- Ananse and Sky-God (Akan)
- Espíritu Pact (Lucumí, the bóveda working)
- Sea-God Deal (West African coastal, Mami Wata bargains)

### 9. Cycle · ◯

Ritual return. Not linear arc but spiral. Time is round. Found in
agricultural societies, liturgical calendars, cosmological systems
where the world resets and returns.

Variants:
- Yoruba Ritual Year (orisha festival cycle)
- Hindu Kalpa (cosmological reset)
- Liturgical Year (Catholic / Lucumí ileche)
- Agricultural Year (universal, planting-to-harvest)
- Eternal Return (Nietzschean / Buddhist)

### 10. Witness · ◉

Testimony as form. The story is the carrying — survival made
narrative. Where the act of telling is itself the act. Black and
diasporic traditions are this archetype's deepest well.

Variants:
- Slave Narrative (African American)
- Calypso Testimonial (Trinidadian, Mighty Sparrow / Roaring Lion)
- Ìtàn (Yoruba, lineage-as-witness)
- Oral History (universal practice)
- Hip-Hop Bars-as-Witness (modern African American, Nas / Kendrick)

## How a creator uses the system

1. On a Cube detail page, pick a primary trajectory. The Cube is
   a *Going*, or a *Lineage*, or a *Refusal*. This is the spine.
2. Optionally pin a specific variant for cultural texture
   (Sankofa under *Return*, Kalfou under *Crossing*).
3. Beats / scenes / arcs apply within the chosen trajectory's
   shape. The library of ten is the shelf; what's built lives on
   the Cube.
4. Stack two trajectories per Cube if needed (a Lineage that
   becomes a Refusal). Stacking is the exception, not the default.

## Why ten and why these ten

Reduction discipline. Booker has 7, Polti has 36, Tobias has 20.
Ten is the sweet spot — fewer than ten would collapse genuinely
distinct patterns; more than ten begins to subdivide rather than
reveal.

The selection is decolonial because:
- *Witness* exists as a peer to *Going*, not as a sub-category
- *Lineage* sits at the same level as *Quest* (rare)
- *Hold* is a primary, not an aesthetic exception
- *Refusal* is recognised as world-making, not as obstruction
- Western frameworks (Hero's Journey, Three-Act) appear as variants
  *under* primaries, not as primaries themselves

The ten are equally weighted across African, Asian, Western, and
diasporic sources. No tradition is the default.

## What this system is for

- Cube spine selection (the arc this Cube follows)
- Filter for the trajectories library on the studio sidebar
- Branding asset: the ten glyphs are Bóveda's narrative iconography,
  parallel to Subtaste's twelve
- Manifest export field (`trajectory: "Refusal"` lands in the JSON
  bundle)
- Cross-Cube comparison ("which of my Cubes are *Going* and which
  are *Hold*?")

## What this system is not

- Not prescriptive. A Cube can refuse to pick one. A Cube can stack
  two. A Cube can declare itself untrajectoried (default).
- Not exhaustive. The variants list is partial; advisors will add.
- Not Western-canonical. The Hero's Journey is one variant of one
  primary. Don't treat it as the master.

## Open questions

- Should the primaries be English-only, or should some carry their
  source-tradition word as the canonical name? (Kalfou instead of
  Crossing, Sankofa instead of Return.) Decision deferred to user.
- Should *Hold* and *Witness* be one primary instead of two? Both
  are observation-centric. Currently kept separate because *Hold*
  is sustained-presence, *Witness* is carry-the-record; different
  emphasis.
- Should the variants list be open for community contribution under
  the protocol? (Cultural advisor review required.)
