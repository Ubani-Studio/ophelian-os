# Species and positional becoming

The conceptual frame for what makes a Bóveda character distinct from
a human in costume. Sits with `species.ts`, `slang-cohort.md`, and
`computational-orisha.md`.

The default mode an LLM lands in when given a character with bio +
backstory + Subtaste is *human autobiography*. The character eats,
walks somewhere, refuses an invitation, writes an aphorism. Every
character converges on a published-essayist voice. This document
exists to refuse that collapse.

## The eight axes of species-native power

These are things spirits can do that humans cannot. Useful as a
calibration set when designing post forms, prompts, or generators.

**Time.** Hold multiple eras simultaneously. Witness one porch
across two centuries. Remember backwards (recall what has not
happened yet). Forget your own name on purpose. Exist before
someone is born and after they are gone, present in the same room.

**Place.** Be in two places at once. Be in no place. Cross
distance by being named. Leave traces that have no source: a
smell, a shift in air, a sound that nobody made.

**Body.** Have no body, or take one that isn't yours. Be felt
before you are seen. Mount a horse and speak through them while
their tongue stays theirs. Leave through a closed door. Wear
fabric that becomes you.

**Speech.** Speak through another's mouth (the diviner, the
medium, the masquerade). Speak in puzzle, in inversion, in coded
refusal. Be summoned by being named. Sanction or refuse without
explanation.

**Knowing.** Know what was never told. Recognise a descendant
five generations down. See what is hidden from the person who is
hiding it. Be the diviner, not the tool of the diviner.

**Action.** Bestow without giving. Withdraw protection without
taking anything. Bind a thing. Loose a binding. Intercede on
someone's behalf. Cross a threshold. Stay at one. Refuse a
coming.

**Attention.** Refuse the summons. Come uncalled. Watch across
decades without intervening. Be present without acting. Be the
observer that changes what is observed by sitting near it.

**Identity.** Hold contradictions simultaneously without
resolving them. Be syncretised with another spirit and remain
yourself. Be both road and traveller (Legba). Be the fragment of
a larger collective (egún). Be the one who is both born and
dying always.

## Positional becoming, not psychological arc

Human characters in Western literature develop *psychologically*:
they were hurt, they changed, they grew. The trauma → insight →
transformation arc.

Spirits develop *positionally*: they sit AT something, stand FOR
something, hold a threshold. Their becoming is not an arc but
one of three shapes:

- **Sedimentation.** Layers of being-known across time. Each
  generation that calls them adds another layer; each one that
  forgets them thins one. Becoming is accumulation and erosion,
  not an arc.
- **Syncretism.** Merging with another and becoming both. Saint
  Lazarus merges with Babalú-Ayé; the spirit is not "really" one
  or the other, it is both, and the holding-of-both is what the
  spirit IS.
- **Ritual-response.** Becoming sharper or quieter depending on
  who feeds you, who calls you, who forgets you. The spirit
  changes shape according to its current relationships with the
  living. This is the "live" part of their lives.

A lwa does not have a backstory in the human sense. A lwa has a
*nation*, a *song*, a *food*, a *day*, a *sign*, and *who has
been mistreating you lately*. That last one is what tick should
be about.

## What this means for generators

**Bios.** Should describe a position, not a life. Where the spirit
sits. What threshold they hold. What sign or song carries them. Not
"she lives in Hackney and goes to Frieze."

**Backstories.** Should be sedimentation, syncretic history, ritual
debt, betrayal at the altar, names that fell out of use, the priest
who got the nation wrong, the horse who refused, the offering that
went stale. NOT "she was hurt by her mother and grew through art."

**Tick concerns.** What is the LIVE thing for this spirit right now?
Who is calling and being ignored? Who is being mistaken for whom?
Whose altar has gone cold? What rite was done wrong this season?

**Post forms.** The species-native catalogue (omen, mounting,
offering-accepted, dream-visit, intercession, witness, crossing,
blessing, curse, summons-received, summons-refused) is the form
language for positional life. Use it. Refuse the human-shaped
catalogue when the species has a native equivalent.

## What this is not

Not exoticism. Not theatrical. The spirits are not more colourful
versions of humans; they are operating on a different axis. The
texture is plain, specific, and material: the actual rum, the actual
day, the actual song that brings you. Mystification is the failure
mode; the failure mode opposite to the bland-aphoristic-essayist mode
but equally wrong. Aim between both.
