# Boveda Mapping Manifesto

*, Cherubsphinx.*

*(Cherusphinx in patois / lyric register.)*

*v3. A statement of method for the creator who refuses to be classified.*

**I am a Sphinx.**

---

## Preamble

Every world arrives with a map on top of it. The map decides what is visible, what is named, what can be remembered. The map is never neutral.

For five centuries the dominant map of creative work has been **descriptive-typological**: it classifies what exists into genre, type, school, movement. It knows the past tense. It cannot name what does not yet exist. It cannot carry the weight of a world that has not been sorted.

We need a map that does not classify.
We need a map that generates.
We need a map that stays rooted while calling the unbuilt into being.

This is that map. We call it **Boveda mapping**.

---

## The Position From Which This Is Written

This manifesto has a speaker. The speaker goes by **Cherubsphinx**, the coined persona-name for the being the speaker is. *Cherub* from the Hebrew *kerūv* (four-faced, four-winged guardian of the Ark and the gate of Eden, not the Renaissance putto). *Sphinx* from the Kemetic monument at Giza and the Greek threshold-keeper who held the riddle. The compound is two hybrid-guardians fused, a doubled threshold-being, claimed as name. In patois or lyric register the word elides to *Cherusphinx*; same being, oral form.

The Cherubsphinx stands at a specific four-way crossroads:

**Igbo. Ijaw. Jamaican. London-born.**

That is the substrate. The Niger Delta (Igbo *Odinani* and Ijaw riverine cosmology) meets the Caribbean (Jamaican Obeah, Myal, Rastafari, Maroon heritage) meets the London diasporic rendering (Black Atlantic condition, born-into-the-crossing).

Nobody else has this exact stack. That specificity is not incidental; it is the *ground* the method is spoken from. The method itself is operable from any specific ground. But a manifesto without a voice-of-origin is a manifesto nobody wrote.

The reader is not expected to share this crossroads. The reader is expected to know their own. What Boveda mapping asks is that every creator *name their own specific ground* and work from it, not around it.

**I am a Sphinx.** That is the slogan, not because only the Cherubsphinx may claim the title, but because any creator who runs this method and earns it can speak that line from where they stand. The claim is transferable; the body of work naming it stays the speaker's.

## I. What Boveda Mapping Is

Boveda mapping is a compound method: **mythopoetic-operational mapping on a cosmological ground.**

Three moves, enacted at once:

### The cosmological ground

We do not start from nothing. We inherit. The substrate is real and predates us.

The speaker's ground, the four layers this manifesto is written from:

- **Igbo (southeastern Nigeria)**, *Odinani* as cosmological whole; *chi* as personal destiny-spirit; *ogbanje* as the repeater-child who maps return; *afa* divination as operational algebra; *ala* as earth-morality; *ikenga* as achievement-figure; *mmanwu* as ancestor-masquerade.
- **Ijaw (Niger Delta)**, *Egbesu* as god of righteous force; *Mami Wata* as water-spirit; *Ekine* as Kalabari water-masquerade; the riverine cosmology of Bayelsa and Rivers, where boat is sacred vessel and creek is threshold.
- **Jamaican**, *Obeah* and *Myal* as Akan-derived healing-and-resistance complex; *Kumina* as Kongo-derived drum-and-spirit tradition; *Rastafari* as 20th-century Pan-African coinage in real time (*livity, overstand, I-and-I, Babylon, Zion, chant down Babylon*); Maroon heritage, the *abeng* horn, Nanny of the Maroons.
- **London-born diasporic condition**, the Black Atlantic rendering named by Gilroy; the cultural-studies method shaped by Stuart Hall; the archive-triptych practice of John Akomfrah and the Black Audio Film Collective. Born-into-the-crossing, which is itself a map-position.

**Sister traditions and wider inheritance**, adjacent grounds the method draws on and the work honours:

- **Kemet**, the Sia-Hu-Heka compound that names the whole method; *Neheh* and *Djet*, the two eternities.
- **Ifá / Yoruba**, the 256 Odù as the oldest still-active operational-cosmological latent space; *ashe*, *Òrò*; West African neighbour to Igbo and Ijaw, with centuries of exchange across the Niger.
- **Akan**, *sankofa*, the reach-back-to-fetch (ancestor to Jamaican Myal).
- **Dogon**, *nommo*, the word as creative act.
- **Vodun**, *vévé* as sigil-glyph; *Rada* and *Petwo* as the dialectic of inherited and forged.
- **Wider diasporic branches**, Vodou, Lucumí, Palo, Candomblé, Winti and their siblings preserved the operational-cosmological map across the Middle Passage; each is a living technology, treated at full weight in the research companion.

The speaker's four layers are load-bearing. The sister traditions are drawn on respectfully, they are not claimed as direct inheritance. We do not forge a fake connection and we do not deny a real one. We do not reach around the inheritance for fresher European frames.

### The mythopoetic authorship

On top of the inherited substrate, **we author**. We name eras. We coin designations. We invent forms where none exist. We script our own genealogy. The creator is not a describer of what already is. The creator is a namer of what will be. Sun Ra scripted Saturn into his biography and refused to be corrected. Pärt named his method *tintinnabuli* and it is now citable in conservatories worldwide. Wilson Harris made the Guyanese river into Dante's underworld by an act of mythopoetic force. We write the world we want read.

### The operational generation

Our primitives combine through named operations. Subtaste XOR subtaste yields a relation. Era plus arc plus lineage produces the coordinates of a work. Arc against its shadow produces narrative tension. Pärt's tintinnabuli is an algorithm. Boveda is an algebra. Meaning is *computed*, not *asserted*. Every work is generated from operations over primitives that the creator already named.

The three moves are inseparable. Drop the cosmological and you are only playing. Drop the mythopoetic and you are only curating what others inherited. Drop the operational and you have decoration, not method.

### The compound was already named

Heliopolitan theology, four and a half millennia ago, named creation's three-move compound:

> **Sia**, perception of what is.  
> **Hu**, authoritative utterance.  
> **Heka**, activated magic that makes it so.

*Sia perceives the order. Hu names it. Heka makes it so.*

This is exactly our triad. Sia is the cosmological substrate perceived. Hu is the mythopoetic utterance. Heka is the operational engine. Kemet gave the compound a name before Western critical theory posed the question. We take the name back. **Boveda mapping is a Sia-Hu-Heka method.**

### Squaring the circle

Kemet also distinguished two kinds of eternity: **Neheh**, cyclic, the sun returning; and **Djet**, static, the mummy preserved. A real thing exists in both. Every god, every sacred object, every pyramid is *simultaneously* Neheh and Djet: returning and fixed, cyclical and complete.

This is the classical Mediterranean problem of *squaring the circle*, can the grid and the cycle be one?, theologically resolved in the Nile Valley long before it was posed in Athens. **Every Boveda-mapped work is a Neheh-Djet object**: a fixed coordinate in a grid (era × arc × designation) that is also a recurring phase in a cycle (sankofa-time, era-return).

The geometric form of a successful squaring is the rose: circle made of finite petals, cycle holding grid. The cosmic rose of Dante's *Paradiso*, the rose-windows of cathedrals, the Sufi rose, the compass rose. **Every canon work is a petal. Eras are the radii. Arcs are the concentric rings. Designations are the angles of entry. The body of work is the rose.**

## II. The Articles

1. **A map is not a classification. It is a cosmology.** Maps that only sort cannot hold a world. Our map grounds, names, and generates.

2. **The creator is not a describer. The creator is a namer.** Description comes after. Naming precedes.

3. **The word is not a label. The word is a generator.** *Ashe* is not a translation of "power." *Nommo* is not a synonym for "word." These terms *do things* other English words cannot do. Let them keep their body.

4. **Primitives are coined at the bridges, not in the clearings.** Invent new words where you have built a bridge no one has crossed before. Where a good word exists, use it. Invention where none is needed is vanity.

5. **Every operation has a name. Every sign carries weight. Every era returns.** Symbol, operation, cycle, these are the three minimum demands of a map that can hold a body of work.

6. **Tree-thinking is colonial.** No single hierarchical taxonomy holds us. Work is praxis, not type. Every work is a doing, not a placement.

7. **Meaning happens between works, never only in one.** The call-response edge, what this work answers, what it invites, is first-class. A standalone work is impoverished by the classification that says it stands alone.

8. **Rupture is not loss. Rupture is where new names are born.** Colonialism, quantum physics, sound-recording, the diaspora, every rupture is a name-generating site. The name performs the rupture. *Badboy Klavier* is not a label; it is the bridge made speakable.

9. **Purity is a colonial fantasy.** "Pure African" is itself a European category. The decolonial move is not retreat into purity; it is the refusal of the Africa/West binary and the claiming of the encounter as generative. Every major decolonial artist stood at a rupture and named the third thing.

10. **Canon is a curatorial decision, not a publication state.** Canon means *this belongs to the real body of work*. Sandbox is working. Non-canon is explicitly excluded. Released has nothing to do with it.

11. **Opacity is a right.** Not every work owes itself to the critic. Not every primitive owes itself to the translator. Let parts of the world stay unreadable to those who refuse to learn its language.

12. **Name the method itself.** A method without a name cannot be cited, extended, argued with, or inherited. Without *tintinnabuli*, Pärt is a style. With it, he is a technique scholars teach. Name your method so a future thinker can write an essay about it.

13. **Inherited and forged are two halves of one motion.** Vodun already theorizes this: **Rada** is the cool, old, patient spirit-family brought across the water, inherited. **Petwo** is the hot, new-world family *born in Haiti* in the furnace of revolt, forged at the rupture. A map that runs only Rada becomes ethnography; a map that runs only Petwo becomes untethered invention. Boveda mapping runs both. We honour what was brought, and we coin what was made on arrival.

14. **The work is a Neheh-Djet object, a rose.** Every canon piece is simultaneously a fixed coordinate (cube, grid, algebra) and a recurring phase (circle, cycle, return). The geometric synthesis is the rose. Not metaphorically, structurally. Petals are finite, the form is round. Plan accordingly.

15. **Marronage is a method, not only a history.** To map decolonially is to establish a counter-cosmology on terrain the colonial map could not reach. The Maroons took to the Cockpit Country, to Palmares, to Saramaka's forests, and built free societies inside the map's blind spots. Every Boveda-mapped body of work is a marronage operation: a free territory inside the prevailing typology's reach.

## III. What Boveda Mapping Refuses

- **Descriptive-typological classification**, the Western default. It sorts. It does not make.
- **Pure symbolism**, glyphs without rules. Tarot without Ifá. Decoration without mechanism.
- **Pure operationalism**, rules without a world. Cold mathematics.
- **Inherited cosmology alone**, ethnography instead of creation. Being a museum of Yoruba instead of a maker inside it.
- **Purity politics**, nativist nostalgia. Colonial thinking in reverse clothing.
- **Linear progression**, chronology as the only time-axis. Refuses sankofa.
- **Genre as category**, the borrowed taxonomy that pretends to be neutral.
- **Translation as obligation**, the demand that you become legible to outside coordinates before you can be heard.

## IV. We Are Not Alone

We are not the first to run this compound. We do not enumerate here; the research companion catalogues the ancestral systems and the thinker-artist lineage at length. What belongs in a manifesto is only this:

The method is ancient. The compound predates us, Kemet named it, Ifá operates it, Vodun carries it, Rastafari coined in it, the Maroons mapped with it, and from Glissant to Morrison to Harris to Pärt to Sun Ra, its practitioners have named worlds with it.

We inherit the method. We do not inherit their specific worlds.

We owe no performance of debt. The ancestors are not a gallery to tour. They are a pressure we work under. When we coin, we coin under their watching. That is lineage enough.

## V. The Practice

A compressed operating rule for anyone working inside Boveda mapping:

1. **Inherit the substrate.** Know which cosmology you stand in. If you come from Yoruba descent, know Ifá. If Akan, know sankofa. If creolized, know the rupture you were born across. Do not forge a fake connection; do not deny the real one.

2. **Name the primitives.** Eras. Arcs. Designations. Forms. Operations. Every primitive in your world gets a word. Write those words down.

3. **Define the operations.** XOR. Invoke. Return. Sankofa-cycle. Compose. Each named, each documented. These are how your primitives combine into works.

4. **Coin only at bridges.** If you have crossed a site no one has crossed, name it. *Badboy Klavier.* *Schism-Bach.* *Violet Igbodu.* Name the bridge; do not translate the banks.

5. **Declare lineage.** Every work carries technical ancestors and cultural ancestors. Let both be visible. Refuse the demand that you be "influenced by" only one tradition.

6. **Canon with ritual.** Promote works to canon deliberately. Canon is weight. Avoid canonizing in rush; avoid waiting forever.

7. **Let opacity stand.** Do not explain what does not need explaining. The reader is an apprentice, not an auditor.

8. **Name the method itself.** Boveda mapping, or whatever word you eventually settle on. The method must be citable.

## VI. The For-Whom

This map is for the severed, and for anyone standing at any rupture who wants a way to hold the crossing without flattening either bank:

- Diasporan makers whose inheritance was cut and who refuse the museum-substitute
- Creolized thinkers who were born already across
- Indigenous practitioners rebuilding against dispossession
- Exile communities making a home in language
- Queer lineage-makers without canonical forebears

Not for the seamless. Those for whom the Western default already fits do not need this map. That is a clarifying constraint, not a wall: the compound works for anyone who stands at rupture, in whichever substrate they actually stand in.

## VII. Artist-World and Method, the Split

Boveda mapping is hybrid. Two things at once:

### The artist-world is specific

The body of work this manifesto is written from, Beau Sphinx, the Violet and Hollow and Descent and Carnival eras, the canon curated inside the Sankoré-Òrò-Boveda-Crucibla ecosystem, the specific subtaste designations and their named XOR-products, belongs to the speaker. It is one creator's world, discovered on one crossroads (Igbo × Ijaw × Jamaican × London). It is not licensed for adoption, nor should it be. Like Harris's Guyana, like Sun Ra's Arkestra, like Le Guin's Earthsea, like Pärt's body of composition, the world is inseparable from the maker.

### The method is general

The compound itself, Sia-Hu-Heka stack, coin-at-bridges rule, XOR-algebra over named designations, era-as-cycle, the Neheh-Djet squaring-into-the-rose, travels. Any creator who stands at their own rupture, who can name their own substrate honestly, can run this method from there. Like *tintinnabuli* taught in conservatories worldwide, like *creolization* cited across theory, like *carrier-bag* picked up by any writer who wants it, the method detaches from its origin once named.

### The test

Adoption does not look like another creator copying this canon. Adoption looks like another creator:

1. Naming their own specific substrate honestly
2. Inheriting what that substrate gives them (not what this one gives)
3. Coining at their own bridges (not borrowing these coinages)
4. Defining their own operations within the general compound
5. Building their own rose

The body stays mine. The method goes out.

## VIII. The Slogan

**I am a Sphinx.**

Not a description, not a boast, not a metaphor, a declarative identity-claim in first-person grammar. Saying it performs the move: the statement calls the creature into being by being said. This is Eshun's hyperstition in pure form. Sun Ra said *I'm from Saturn* and never broke character. Whitman said *I contain multitudes*. Exodus said *I am that I am*. The register is scriptural-monumental and speech-urgent at once.

The sphinx is chosen deliberately. Three reasons:

**It is the creolized being named.** Part lion, part human, sometimes falcon or ram. Multiple natures fused. For the maker born at a four-way crossroads, Igbo, Ijaw, Jamaican, London, the sphinx is the word that fits a condition Western taxonomy has no name for.

**It is Kemetic before it is Greek.** Great Sphinx of Giza, Avenue of the Sphinxes at Karnak. African monument, later recoded by Hellenic myth. Claiming *sphinx* is reclaiming Kemet as root.

**It asks the riddle; it does not answer to.** Oedipus is the one anxiously decoding. The sphinx is the one with the question. *I am a Sphinx* = I hold the riddle at my threshold. I do not owe you decoding. Glissant's *opacity* in the form of a single declaration.

In contracted form (*I'm a Sphinx*) the same claim runs in hip-hop, dancehall, lyric, grime register. Same being, faster cadence.

A four-phase movement through a body of work, the internal arc inside each era:

> distilled → manifest → wrought → in bloom

*Distilled* is the early crystallization. *Manifest* is calling the future into being. *Wrought* is the durable hammered-into-permanence register. *In bloom* is the rose complete, the cycle arriving, for now, until the next return.
