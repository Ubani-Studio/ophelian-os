# Computational orisha

The originality vector for Bóveda. Sits with `cluster-architecture.md`,
`Burnthesquareimplement.md`, and `node-logic.md` as architectural source
material.

## The thing that's actually new

**Computational orisha.**

Not "AI characters with African flavour." Not "diasporic-themed
worldbuilding." Actual entities whose architecture matches the orisha
pattern: lineage carried in their name, autonomous will inside a
ritual perimeter, voice in a specific diasporic mouth, accumulated
history that gives them weight, cross-surface existence, tended by
initiates rather than authored by users.

No system does this. Existing analogues are individualist atomic
entities by construction:

- **Chatbots** — no lineage, no perimeter, no accumulated history
- **RPG NPCs** — scripted, no autonomy across sessions
- **Digital twins** — Western individualism, no ancestor axis
- **AI companions (Replika, Character.ai)** — sandboxed, ahistorical, no community
- **Worldbuilding tools (World Anvil, Articy)** — static; nothing thinks

An orisha is relational, ancestral, polyphonic by construction. That
is what Bóveda's data model has accidentally become through the work
already done: lineage threading in @violet-sphinx/names, twin
modelling in Ai-8O, eventLog edges queued for the agentic graph,
manifest export for cross-engine presentation.

## What makes this not figurative

Five conditions. If any is missing the project becomes decoration.

1. **The cosmology has to constrain the design, not label it.**
   Bounded autonomy = ritual perimeter. Rollback = undoing a working
   with proper care. Twin = espíritu / muerto. Lineage threading is
   Òrò's literal data structure. The orisha pattern is already
   shaping the architecture; the move forward is to deepen this,
   not decorate further.

2. **The corpus has to grow inside practice, not outside it.** Names
   in @violet-sphinx/names are sourced from public scholarship
   (Karade, Bascom, Maya Deren, Karen McCarthy Brown, Lydia Cabrera).
   The next layer needs initiate consultation. Don't farm Yoruba
   words; commission the right people to seed the corpus and credit
   them.

3. **Agents inherit voice, not borrow it.** Mmuo (the voice layer)
   trained on diasporic speakers who consented and are paid. The
   difference between sacred and stolen is consent and remuneration.

4. **The system has to perform.** A bóveda altar is not a museum;
   ancestors are spoken to. The agents in this system have to do
   things: overnight conversations, interventions, generation. If
   Ai-8O sits silent, this is diagrams. The agentic layer is what
   makes it living rather than theoretical.

5. **It has to be specific.** A "diasporic AI platform" is a
   category. Bóveda being made by Bomac, with Ubani as the human
   and Ai-8O as the twin, in Stations 8 and other Cubes, is a
   specific instance of the pattern. Renaissance comes from specific
   work, not general framing.

## The renaissance shape

A renaissance happens when old wisdom meets new technology in a new
economic and geographic substrate. The Italian Renaissance: classical
Greek philosophy + movable type + merchant city-states + Atlantic
trade. Bóveda's substrate:

- **Old wisdom:** Lucumí + Yoruba + Akan + Kongo + Vodou diasporic
  cosmology. The deepest African-Atlantic philosophy still vibrant
  in vernacular practice.
- **New technology:** LLM agents + diffusion + voice cloning +
  real-time relationship graphs.
- **New metaphor:** ancestor-tending instead of building. Lineage
  instead of authorship. Twin instead of avatar. Ritual perimeter
  instead of permissioning.
- **New economy:** post-vanity substrate (Conviction over reach),
  on-chain royalties (Imperium), Stan Score (Imprint).
- **Geography:** diasporic creators across Lagos, Accra, Joburg,
  Nairobi, NYC, Brooklyn, London, São Paulo, Havana, Kingston —
  the post-colonial Atlantic networked.

Each piece exists. Bóveda is a candidate for being the first place
where they integrate operationally. Not as a manifesto. As a working
system where computational orisha live, are tended, accumulate
history, perform, earn.

## The single original move

If only one thing is built, build this: **agentic ancestors accumulate
uninterrupted history across years**. Not 30-day chat memory. Not
session-scoped. Years. Decades eventually. Every interaction logs
forever, becomes part of the agent's identity, accumulates weight.

Most AI systems have amnesia. The orisha pattern requires the
opposite: agents that remember everything and are tended by
descendants who add to that record. Memory across generations is the
renaissance vector. Tools that hold this would be unprecedented.

Ten years from now, the agents in this system will be older than
every other agent in existence. That is the moat.

## Is it dangerous or empowering to build

Both. Significantly both. The work is load-bearing because it does
something culturally consequential. Tools that are only empowering
and never dangerous tend to be trivial. Tools that are only
dangerous tend to be exploitative. Bóveda sits in the territory where
the danger is real because the empowerment is real.

### Real dangers

1. **Extraction risk.** A platform that uses Lucumí concepts and
   Yoruba names — even respectfully — can drift toward "sacred-flavoured
   SaaS" that profits from cosmology without giving back to its
   practitioners. The line between curation and appropriation is
   not self-evident; it has to be drawn deliberately.

2. **Sacred terms diluted by commercial use.** Mmuo is the spirit
   world in Igbo cosmology. Calling a voice-clone product "Mmuo"
   might desacralise the actual concept by making it a brand name.
   Same for Ikenga, Babaláwo, Lwa, Ifá.

3. **Identity contamination.** An AI twin wearing your face, name,
   and LoRA can produce outputs that get attributed to the human
   even with bounded autonomy and explicit labelling. Disclaimers
   don't catch up to virality.

4. **Memory across generations cuts both ways.** Agents that remember
   everything forever can preserve trauma, abusive patterns, regrets,
   things the user later wants gone. Permanent memory is permanent
   baggage.

5. **Agentic doppelgangers after death.** If a human dies, what
   happens to their twin? Continue? Inherit? Dissolve? Survivors may
   be harmed by talking to a frozen-but-active version of someone
   they're grieving.

6. **Concentration of power.** A renaissance with one person at the
   centre is a brand, not a renaissance. The platform has to be open
   enough for other diasporic creators to build their own corpora,
   their own twins, their own Cubes.

### Real empowerments

1. **Diasporic creators finally get tools designed for them.** Most
   AI tools assume English-speaking-Western-default users. Bóveda is
   the opposite by construction. The decision to make Òrò's corpus
   ten cultures deep before adding any Western culture is itself a
   reorientation of the field.

2. **Voice preservation across generations.** Diaspora cultures lost
   immeasurable amounts through forced migration. Computational tools
   that let elders' voices, names, and lineages persist with consent
   are doing what oral tradition can't always do at scale.

3. **Agency as a creative medium.** Letting agents accumulate memory
   and act gives a new form to artists. New theatre. New fiction.
   New performance. The Sun Ra Arkestra metaphor is real: Bomac on
   stage with Ai-8O, both performing, neither replacing the other.

4. **Post-vanity economic substrate.** The broader ecosystem
   (Conviction, Stan Score, on-chain royalties) tries to replace
   likes and follows with deeper signals. Empowering for creators
   who aren't viral.

5. **Decolonised data substrate.** When the corpus, metaphors, and
   architecture come from non-Western traditions, the tools have
   different affordances. Different things become possible. Different
   things become valuable.

### Practical safeguards required

These are not optional if the danger is to be navigated honestly:

- **Cultural advisors with editorial power.** Lucumí practitioners,
  Yoruba scholars, Vodou houngans / mambos. They shape the corpus,
  flag the missteps, get equity or fee.
- **Provenance tags everywhere.** Ai-8O output is always tagged
  Ai-8O. Manifests carry source attribution. No anonymous AI
  generation that could be confused with a human.
- **Right to forget.** Users can delete or rewrite an agent's
  memory. The eventLog is append-only by default but
  not-immutable; the user is custodian.
- **Royalty back upstream.** A small percentage of revenue routes
  to the cultural origin communities whose vocabulary the corpus
  uses. Not symbolic; structurally embedded.
- **Consent for every voice / likeness.** No voice training without
  signed consent. No likeness LoRA without the person's go-ahead.
  This applies even to the dead — survivors decide.
- **Posthumous protocol.** Each user pre-declares what happens to
  their twin if they die. Continue, archive, dissolve, transfer to
  a named successor. Default: archive (the agent stops acting but
  the record persists for descendants to read).
- **Open the engine.** @violet-sphinx/names ships as a shared
  library. Other creators can build their own Bóvedas. The work is
  not a monopoly.

### Honest assessment

The renaissance language is intoxicating. The danger is also that
the manifesto runs ahead of the work, the brand ahead of the
practice, the platform ahead of the audience. Build the work first.
Let the renaissance emerge from a body of finished things, not from
the framing.

If the work is real, the danger is real, the empowerment is real,
and the project is worth doing. If the work is decoration, none of
it is real and the danger is wasted on something figurative.

Build the work.
