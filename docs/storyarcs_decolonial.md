# Story arcs · decolonial

The canonical Bóveda trajectory system. Ten primary arcs, grounded in
Soyinka's fourth-stage cosmology and validated against post-colonial
African, Caribbean, and diasporic literature, with named variants
drawn from African, Asian, Western, and Black-Atlantic sources so
that no single tradition is the default.

This file replaces the earlier `trajectories.md` draft. The earlier
ten (Going / Trick / Lineage / Return / Refusal / Hold / Crossing /
Pact / Cycle / Witness) is preserved as a research lineage; what
survives from it is the brand-discipline (one word per primary, one
glyph per primary) and the variants layer (Asian and Western
traditions appear as named variants under each primary, not erased
and not centered).

The primary set is the brand. The variants are the lineage. A
creator picks a primary at the Cube level (this Cube is a *Descent*)
and optionally pins a specific variant for cultural texture
(a *Descent* in the fourth-stage tradition; a *Return* in the
Sankofa tradition).

## Why ten and why these ten

Reduction discipline. Booker has 7. Polti has 36. Tobias has 20.
Ten is the sweet spot: fewer than ten collapses genuinely distinct
patterns; more than ten begins to subdivide rather than reveal.

The ten are Soyinka-anchored, post-colonial-validated, and
cross-culturally stress-tested:

1. Origin · ⊙
2. Descent · ⤓
3. Return · ↺
4. Liberation · ⇡
5. Communion · ⌬
6. Encounter · ✛
7. Inheritance · ⫷
8. Sacrifice · ⊗
9. Trickster · ⌘
10. Exile · ⤳

Mirrors Subtaste's twelve glyphs as a parallel iconography.

## Cosmological grounding · the fourth stage

Soyinka's "fourth stage" provides the deepest theoretical anchor.
Yoruba cosmology recognises three structured realms (the world of
ancestors *Orun*, of the living *Ayé*, of the unborn). The fourth
stage is the *transitional void* between them, a zone of chaos
where categories dissolve.

Ogun, the iron god, is patron of tragic action because he alone
crossed this abyss. His duality (creator and destroyer, metallurgy
and war) makes him the archetype of will-in-passage. Soyinka:
*"Ogun is the embodiment of Will, and the Will is the paradoxical
truth of destructiveness and creativeness in acting man."*

Three primaries are direct fourth-stage arcs: **Descent** (the
passage), **Sacrifice** (what enables the passage), **Return**
(what comes back from it). Two more sit at the abyss-edge:
**Encounter** (contact across the void), **Exile** (permanent
displacement from the line).

The remaining five (**Origin**, **Liberation**, **Communion**,
**Inheritance**, **Trickster**) are the load-bearing community
arcs: how a people begins, frees itself, gathers, transmits, and
outwits.

## Vodun temperature classification

Each primary carries a temperature drawn from Vodun ritual
classification (Rada cool, Petwo hot, Kalfou crossroads). The
temperature is a tuning hint for tone, pacing, and how a
trajectory wants to be performed.

| Primary | Temperature | Motion | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Origin | cool | settling | gathering |
| Descent | hot | falling | dissolving |
| Return | cool | spiraling | resolving |
| Liberation | hot | rising | breaking |
| Communion | cool | encircling | balancing |
| Encounter | crossroads | meeting | charging |
| Inheritance | cool | descending-line | weighting |
| Sacrifice | hot | offering | transmuting |
| Trickster | crossroads | sidestepping | inverting |
| Exile | crossroads | drifting | suspending |

Hot arcs move through transformation by force. Cool arcs move
through accumulation and balance. Crossroads arcs hold contradiction
without resolving it.

## The ten primaries

Each entry has the same shape: definition, five-phase structure,
compatible secondaries, shadow type, and named variants.

### 1. Origin · ⊙

Stories of beginnings. Creation, first migration, the *recovery* of
erased pasts. Often *excavated* rather than told, since the past
must be actively rescued from colonial erasure.

**Five phases**
1. Void / chaos
2. Spark / word / first breath
3. Differentiation
4. Ordering / naming
5. Inhabitation

**Compatible secondaries:** Inheritance, Communion, Encounter
**Shadow:** Exile (the origin you cannot return to)

**Variants**
- Olódùmarè and the calabash (Yoruba)
- Atum's self-creation (Kemetic)
- Enuma Elish (Sumerian)
- Nyame and the spider's first tale (Akan)
- Hiranyagarbha · golden womb (Hindu)
- Wújí to Tàijí (Daoist)
- Dreamtime emergence (Aboriginal Australian)
- Genesis (Hebraic)
- Brathwaite's Arrivants (excavation-as-origin, diasporic)
- Okri's abiku choosing human life (modern Yoruba)

### 2. Descent · ⤓

Passage through liminal space. Crisis, dissolution, the fourth
stage. The protagonist enters the abyss of transition and emerges
transformed or destroyed. Soyinka's master pattern.

**Five phases**
1. Threshold-call
2. Crossing-down
3. Abyss / dissolution
4. Confrontation
5. Re-emergence (or annihilation)

**Compatible secondaries:** Sacrifice, Return, Encounter
**Shadow:** Communion (the corrupted ritual, the perverted feast)

**Variants**
- Ogun's fourth-stage passage (Yoruba, Soyinka)
- Inanna's descent (Sumerian)
- Orpheus (Greek)
- Middle Passage (Black Atlantic, structural)
- The Road (Soyinka)
- Buddhist hell realms (Naraka)
- Dante's Inferno (Christian medieval)
- Sipapu emergence-from-below (Hopi)
- Limbo gateway (Wilson Harris, Caribbean)

### 3. Return · ↺

Movement toward homeland: physical, spiritual, psychological, or
*impossible*. The returning self is changed. Includes failed
returns where home no longer exists. Yoruba *Atunwa* gives this
its cyclical depth: souls return continuously, names like
Babatunde encode the belief.

**Five phases**
1. Distance / exile-state
2. Call / pull
3. Journey-back
4. Threshold of arrival
5. Reconciliation (or refusal of welcome)

**Compatible secondaries:** Origin, Inheritance, Communion
**Shadow:** Exile (the homecoming that cannot complete)

**Variants**
- Odyssey (Greek)
- Sankofa · go back to fetch (Akan)
- Ramayana banishment-return (Indian)
- Atunwa (Yoruba reincarnation as return)
- Prodigal (Hebraic)
- A Dance of the Forests (Soyinka, ancestors returning)
- Diasporic return (modern African and Caribbean homecoming)
- Cyclical return / time-as-round (Yoruba ritual year, Hindu Kalpa,
  liturgical year, agricultural year, eternal return)

### 4. Liberation · ⇡

Struggle against bondage toward freedom. Achieved, ongoing, or
frustrated. Encompasses the full arc from chains to break, whether
or not the break holds.

**Five phases**
1. Bondage / unfreedom
2. Recognition
3. Struggle / resistance
4. Break
5. After-the-break (the new condition, with its own weight)

**Compatible secondaries:** Sacrifice, Encounter, Trickster
**Shadow:** Inheritance (the chains carried from the line)

**Variants**
- Mau Mau / Dedan Kimathi (Kenyan, Ngũgĩ)
- Maroon flight (Caribbean / South American)
- Abolitionist arc (African American)
- Satyagraha (Gandhi, Indian)
- Caliban in *A Tempest* (Césaire, Caribbean)
- Spartacus (Roman)
- Exodus (Hebraic)
- Haitian Revolution
- Bhagavad Gita's call to action (Indian)
- Anti-bildungsroman · refusal-as-liberation (modern Western)

### 5. Communion · ⌬

Coming together in community, solidarity, or ritual. Includes
*failed* communion where ritual is corrupted and gathering
fractures, since post-colonial literature shows perverted communion
as often as successful communion.

**Five phases**
1. Dispersal / isolation
2. Call to gather
3. Convergence
4. Ritual / shared act
5. Communion held (or broken)

**Compatible secondaries:** Inheritance, Return, Origin
**Shadow:** Exile (the communion you cannot enter)

**Variants**
- Egungun festival (Yoruba)
- Lucumí ileche / liturgical year (Afro-Cuban)
- Sangha (Buddhist gathering)
- Quaker meeting (silent gathering)
- Catholic mass
- Brathwaite tidealectic gathering (Caribbean)
- Failed feast / corrupted ritual (Soyinka, *Madmen and Specialists*)
- Hold · stillness as gathering (Japanese kishōtenketsu, kabuki
  mié, slow cinema, contemplative tale, vigil)

### 6. Encounter · ✛

Contact between cultures, worldviews, or individuals across
difference. Colonial first contact, tradition meeting modernity,
the self confronting the Other. Includes *internalised* encounter
where the colonised absorbs the coloniser's gaze.

**Five phases**
1. Separation (two worlds apart)
2. Approach
3. Contact
4. Collision / charge / mutual change
5. Aftermath (and what each side carries forward)

**Compatible secondaries:** Descent, Exile, Trickster, Liberation
**Shadow:** Inheritance (the closed circle that refuses the other)

**Variants**
- Things Fall Apart (Achebe, Igbo / colonial)
- *A Tempest* · Caliban-Prospero (Césaire)
- *Master Harold and the Boys* (Fugard, apartheid)
- *Americanah* (Adichie, bicultural)
- *Death and the King's Horseman* (Soyinka)
- Krishna-Arjuna dialogue (Mahabharata)
- Silk Road encounter (Tang dynasty Chinese)
- Kalfou crossroads (Vodou, encounter as cosmology)

### 7. Inheritance · ⫷

What is passed down across generations. Ancestral debt, colonial
legacy, transmitted trauma and memory, sacred obligation. The
"nervous conditions" children inherit from parents shaped by
oppression.

**Five phases**
1. Ancestral act
2. Transmission
3. Reception (the inheritor recognising the load)
4. Carrying (or refusing to carry)
5. Passing on (or breaking the line)

**Compatible secondaries:** Origin, Return, Communion, Sacrifice
**Shadow:** Liberation (the breaking from the line)

**Variants**
- Mahabharata · Kuru lineage (Indian)
- Ifá Odu pattern · 256 odu (Yoruba divinatory)
- Oríkì praise-lineage (Yoruba praise poetry)
- Roots (Haley, African American)
- Begats (Hebraic biblical)
- *Nervous Conditions* (Dangarembga, Zimbabwean)
- *Beloved* (Morrison, postmemory)
- Confucian filial piety (Chinese)
- Witness · testimony as inherited record (slave narrative,
  Ìtàn, calypso, hip-hop bars)

### 8. Sacrifice · ⊗

Giving up something precious for communal or cosmic benefit. The
carrier, the scapegoat, the willing self-offering versus imposed
victimhood. Central to Yoruba tragic structure. Connected to
*àṣẹ* (life force enabling transition between worlds).

**Five phases**
1. Recognition of the cost
2. Resistance (often, *Death and the King's Horseman*)
3. Acceptance
4. Offering
5. Cosmic exchange / restoration

**Compatible secondaries:** Descent, Inheritance, Liberation
**Shadow:** Trickster (cunning escape vs willing offering)

**Variants**
- *The Strong Breed* (Soyinka, the carrier)
- Elesin Oba (Soyinka, *Death and the King's Horseman*)
- Christ's passion (Christian)
- Iphigenia (Greek)
- Bhishma's vow (Mahabharata)
- Dedan Kimathi as martyr (Ngũgĩ)
- Aztec Tlazolteotl
- Pact · contracted exchange with non-human force (Faustian
  bargain, blues crossroads / Robert Johnson, Mami Wata sea-god
  deal, Espíritu pact / Lucumí bóveda working, Ananse and sky-god)

### 9. Trickster · ⌘

The cunning figure who disrupts order, resists power through wit,
and transforms constraints into opportunities. Anansi, Èṣù-Legba,
Sun Wukong, Ti-Jean, Coyote, Baroka. *Counter-hegemonic agency*:
the weak defeating the powerful through intelligence rather than
strength. Anansi carried coded resistance across the Middle
Passage.

**Five phases**
1. Constraint / power-imbalance
2. Recognition (the trickster sees the gap)
3. Cunning / deception
4. Inversion
5. Aftermath (often morally ambiguous)

**Compatible secondaries:** Encounter, Liberation, Exile
**Shadow:** Sacrifice (cunning escape vs willing offering)

**Variants**
- Ananse cycle (Akan)
- Èṣù crossroads (Yoruba)
- Br'er Rabbit (African American)
- Ti-Jean (Caribbean)
- Pícaro (Spanish picaresque)
- Heist (modern Western)
- Sun Wukong · Monkey King (Chinese)
- Coyote (Native American)
- Hermes (Greek)
- Loki (Norse)
- Baroka (Soyinka, *The Lion and the Jewel*)

### 10. Exile · ⤳

Permanent displacement from homeland without possibility of return.
The condition of "unhomeliness" (Bhabha): domestic spaces become
sites of history's invasions. Said's "situatedness-in-displacement":
self-knowledge produced through separation rather than reunion.
Distinct from Return because no homecoming is available.

**Five phases**
1. Severance (forced or chosen)
2. Threshold-out
3. Drift / suspension
4. Settling-into-non-home
5. Production of self-from-distance

**Compatible secondaries:** Encounter, Trickster, Descent
**Shadow:** Return (the homecoming refused or impossible)

**Variants**
- Naipaul's exile consciousness (Trinidadian / British)
- Bhabha's unhomeliness (theoretical)
- Walcott's not-quite-return (St. Lucian)
- Adam expelled (Hebraic)
- Ovid in Tomis (Roman)
- Tibetan exile / Dharamsala (modern)
- Bartleby (Melville, refusal-becomes-exile)
- Diasporic permanence (Black Atlantic, modern)

## How a creator uses the system

1. On a Cube detail page, pick a primary trajectory. The Cube is a
   *Descent*, or a *Return*, or an *Exile*. This is the spine.
2. Optionally pin a specific variant for cultural texture (Sankofa
   under *Return*, the Strong Breed under *Sacrifice*, kishōtenketsu
   under *Communion*).
3. Read the temperature. *Hot* arcs want kinetic pacing and forced
   transformation. *Cool* arcs want accumulation and balance.
   *Crossroads* arcs want contradiction held without resolution.
4. Beats / scenes / arcs apply within the chosen trajectory's
   five-phase shape. The library of ten is the shelf; what's
   built lives on the Cube.
5. Stack two trajectories per Cube if needed. Use the compatibility
   table: *Inheritance* + *Liberation* is the shadow stack (the
   line you must break to be free), structurally generative. The
   shadow row is for stories that hold their own contradiction.
6. Stacking is the exception, not the default.

## What this system is for

- Cube spine selection (the arc this Cube follows)
- Filter for the trajectories library on the studio sidebar
- Branding asset: the ten glyphs are Bóveda's narrative iconography,
  parallel to Subtaste's twelve
- Manifest export field (`trajectory: "Descent"`, optional
  `variant: "Sankofa"`, `temperature: "hot"`) lands in the JSON
  bundle for engine consumption
- Cross-Cube comparison ("which of my Cubes are *Descent* and
  which are *Communion*?")
- Beat-template seeding (the five-phase structure becomes the
  default scene scaffold for the chosen primary)

## What this system is not

- Not prescriptive. A Cube can refuse to pick one. A Cube can stack
  two. A Cube can declare itself untrajectoried (default).
- Not exhaustive. The variants list is partial; cultural advisors
  add more under the protocol.
- Not Western-canonical. Hero's Journey, Three-Act, and Faustian
  Bargain appear only as variants, not as primaries.
- Not Yoruba-canonical either. Soyinka's fourth stage is the
  theoretical anchor because it gives the deepest cosmological
  ground for the abyss arcs, but the ten stress-test across
  Kemetic, Sumerian, Akan, Yoruba, Hindu, Daoist, Chinese, Greek,
  Hebraic, Caribbean, and Black-Atlantic sources.

## What we kept and what we dropped

From the earlier `trajectories.md` draft we kept:
- One word per primary
- One glyph per primary
- The variants layer (Western and Asian traditions appear as named
  variants, not erased and not centered)
- The "stack two if needed, default is one" rule

What changed:
- *Going* dropped. Hero's Journey now appears as a variant under
  *Liberation* (the break-from-village arc) or *Encounter*
  (depending on framing). The "journey out" is not its own primary;
  it is a phase of *Descent*, *Liberation*, or *Encounter*.
- *Hold* folded into *Communion*. Stillness-as-gathering is real,
  but it is a *form* of communion (the silent meeting, the vigil,
  the kabuki mié) rather than a parallel primary.
- *Cycle* folded into *Return*. Cyclical / spiral time is named
  explicitly under *Return* with Yoruba ritual year, Hindu Kalpa,
  liturgical year, agricultural year, and eternal return as
  variants.
- *Pact* folded into *Sacrifice*. Faustian bargain, blues
  crossroads, Mami Wata, Espíritu pact, Ananse-and-sky-god are
  contracted variants of sacrifice (one-way offering with a
  reciprocal contract attached).
- *Refusal* split. Bartleby goes under *Exile*, satyagraha under
  *Liberation*, anti-bildungsroman under *Liberation* or
  *Trickster* by case.
- *Crossing* renamed *Encounter* and broadened. Threshold-between-
  worlds (Kalfou, Charon's ferry, Òrun-Ayé) becomes a variant
  cluster within *Encounter* alongside cross-cultural contact.
- *Witness* demoted to a variant under *Inheritance* (testimony as
  the inherited record). See open question below.

What we gained:
- *Origin*. Creation, first migration, the *excavation* of erased
  pasts. Real shape, missing from the earlier draft.
- *Descent*. Soyinka's master pattern. Fourth-stage passage.
  Foundational; was half-covered by *Crossing* + *Pact* in the
  earlier draft and now stands as its own primary.
- *Liberation*. Struggle-with-aim is structurally distinct from
  refusal-as-form. The earlier draft's *Refusal* was too clever.
- *Communion*. Coming together is a real arc, even when it fails.
  The earlier draft missed it.
- *Encounter*. Contact across difference is broader and more
  load-bearing than the earlier *Crossing*.
- *Exile*. Permanent displacement is structurally distinct from
  *Return*. The earlier draft collapsed them. This is the
  Said-Bhabha-Naipaul shape.
- The five-phase scaffold per primary, the temperature
  classification, and the compatibility / shadow matrix.

## Open questions

- **Witness as primary (instead of Communion or Inheritance).**
  Slave narrative, Ìtàn, calypso testimonial, hip-hop bars treat
  *the telling as the act*. Currently filed as a variant under
  *Inheritance* (testimony-as-inherited-record). The argument for
  promotion: diasporic centrality. The argument against: it
  functions as a *mode* applicable across primaries (one can
  witness within Return, Descent, Inheritance) more often than as
  a structural spine. Decision deferred to user.
- **Communion as primary or as resolution-phase.** The
  *failed-communion* pattern (Soyinka's perverted feast, Kongi's
  appropriated harvest) suggests Communion may function as the
  *resolution phase* of *Descent* or *Liberation* more often than
  as a spine. Kept as primary for now because gathering-as-spine
  does occur (sangha tales, potlatch, Brathwaite's tidealectic).
  Decision deferred to user.
- **Healing/Memory as primaries.** Both flagged in the source
  research as candidates. Both currently fold into *Descent*
  (healing as resolution phase) and *Inheritance* (memory as the
  thing inherited). Could promote either if the variants list
  bursts at the seams.
- **Should primaries be English-only, or should some carry their
  source-tradition word as the canonical name?** Sankofa instead
  of Return. Kalfou instead of Encounter. Decision deferred to
  user.
- **Should the variants list be open for community contribution
  under the protocol?** Cultural-advisor review required if so.
