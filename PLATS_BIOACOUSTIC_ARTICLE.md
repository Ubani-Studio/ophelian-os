## I. The field

Most of what is alive is not seen and not heard.

Elephants speak through dirt at five hertz. Frog embryos hatch
early when they feel the wasp on the leaf above them. A coral reef
chorusing at full health sounds like a kitchen at dinner; bleached,
it goes silent in a single season. The forest soil in your hand
contains more species than the visible animals of an ecoregion.
The sun has a sound. Saturn's magnetosphere has a song. A pulsar
ticks more regularly than every atomic clock on earth combined.

These are not metaphors. Each is a *measurable signal* in a
recognised scientific subfield. They have been there the whole
time. They have not been named as one field.

PLATS – Bioacoustic is the name for the discipline that names them
as one field, builds the instruments to work with them, refuses
the colonial gaze that buried them, and insists they enter the
practice of the working artist alongside the practice of the
working ecologist.

The discipline is new only in being named. The work is older than
any of us.

## III. The phenomena, named

These are real. Each has a research literature, a measurement
protocol, a public dataset, a working community.

### Infrasonic elephant communication

The Cornell Elephant Listening Project, founded by Katy Payne,
has been recording African forest elephants for thirty years.
Their rumbles between five and thirty-five hertz travel ten
kilometres through air and farther through soil. Caitlin
O'Connell-Rodwell showed that elephants detect ground-borne
signals through pressure-sensitive cells in their feet and trunk
tip. They hear with their bodies. They are talking to each other
through the ground beneath us.

Bring a geophone. Speed-shift the recording forty times. You hear
a register your work can sit inside. The infrasonic register is
not new. The instrument to enter it as a working artist is.

### Seismic substrate transmission

Karen Warkentin showed red-eyed tree frog embryos hatch early
when they feel the vibrational signature of a wasp landing on the
leaf above their egg cluster. Peggy Hill catalogued vibrational
communication across insect orders — treehoppers, leafhoppers,
stink bugs — talking to each other through stem and soil. Most
animal communication is not airborne. Most animal communication
is not human-audible. Most animal communication is below.

The phrase you want for the article is *substrate ecology*. It is
real. It is documented. It is missing from contemporary creative
practice because the instrument to work with it has not existed.

### Whale codas

Hal Whitehead spent forty years documenting that sperm whale
matrilineal clans carry culture. Each clan has its own click
rhythm, a *coda*, distinct from every other clan, transmitted
mother to calf, persistent over generations. Cetacean culture is
multi-generational. Cetacean dialect is geographically organised.
Cetacean clans have a structural lineage equivalent to ifa odu —
a finite set of distinguishable cipher-figures that organise
identity.

The Christopher Clark recordings at Cornell's Bioacoustics
Research Program contain forty years of blue whale calls across
ocean basins. Public. Free. Sitting on a server, waiting to be
worked with.

### Coral reef chorus

Steve Simpson at Exeter and Tim Lamont at Lancaster proved that a
healthy coral reef sounds like a kitchen. The snapping shrimp
clicking, the territorial fish honking, the parrotfish scraping
substrate. When the reef bleaches, the chorus collapses in a
single season. Reef audio is *climate evidence in the present
tense*. The reef tells you it is dying. The instrument to listen
is a hydrophone you can buy for £200.

### Volcanic tremor

The USGS Hawaiian Volcano Observatory runs broadband seismometers
that catch sustained harmonic tremor between 0.01 and 100 hertz.
Tremor predicts eruption. At one hundred times normal speed the
tremor becomes a drone instrument with strict physical provenance.
Mount Yasur's basso continuo. The PERI nn~ patch already speaks
this register and the writer's father's voice is in the same
patch's training set. The lineage is technically composable.

### Glacial cracking

EastGRIP and the Drift+Noise pipeline put cryoseismometers into
Greenland ice. Every calving event is measured to the second. The
audio is the climate's testimony in real time. Music that is also
receipt.

### Bat echolocation

Pipistrelle bats peak at 45 kilohertz. Horseshoe bats higher.
BatDetect2 and Tadarida identify species by call pattern. Time-
expand the recording ten times and the rainforest at dusk
becomes a chorus you already knew was there. The simplest queer-
ecological intervention available: the unheard becomes heard.

### Soil microbiome

The EarthMicrobiome Project has sequenced soils across the globe.
A teaspoon of forest soil contains more bacterial species than
the visible animals of an ecoregion. Sonifying microbial
community diversity is sonifying the most species-dense system on
the planet. The article should name how absurd it is that we
still call this *dirt*.

### eDNA — the ghost census

Filter ten litres of pond water through a Sterivex filter.
Sequence the captured DNA. Learn what fish, amphibians, mammals
passed through that water in the last weeks. The Cornell eDNA
Initiative and NatureMetrics make the protocols accessible.

Present-absence. The animal was here. You did not see it. You can
prove it. The political stakes are enormous: eDNA is how
biodiversity inventory begins to scale. eDNA is also how
conservation work becomes possible at the scale extinction is
happening.

### Pulsar timing arrays

NANOGrav, the European Pulsar Timing Array, and the Indian
Pulsar Timing Array measure pulsar arrival times across the
galaxy. Their data is public. Their measurements are more regular
than every clock on earth combined. LIGO's gravitational-wave
chirps are public domain. NASA heliophysics publishes the solar
wind in real time.

The sun has a sound. We can listen.

## IV. The instruments

PLATS-Bioacoustic is not a metaphor. It is an instrument stack
that produces working artefacts.

### Layer 1 — Field signal

Forest, lake, reef, wetland, glacier, cave, marsh, city, body,
machine, archive. Each is a signal source. Each carries a
documented capture protocol from a documented research tradition.

### Layer 2 — Scientific capture

AudioMoth recorders at ninety pounds. Aquarian H2a hydrophones.
Geophone GS-11D for substrate seismic. LOM Geofón LMG for art-
world field work. Wildlife Acoustics SongMeter Mini Bat for
ultrasonic. Aurelia eDNA filter samplers. DJI L1 LiDAR for site
photogrammetry. Sentinel-2 satellite imagery free for the
asking. Pulsar timing archives public.

These are real prices. These are real protocols. They are not
hypothetical.

### Layer 3 — Intelligence

GBIF for global biodiversity occurrence. iNaturalist for citizen
observation. xeno-canto for the world's largest bird recording
archive, all under Creative Commons. Macaulay Library at Cornell
for animal audio + video. OBIS for marine biodiversity. Map of
Life for range maps. IUCN Red List for conservation status.
scikit-maad in Python for the soundscape ecology indices: ACI,
NDSI, BI, ADI, AEI, H. BirdNET-Analyzer for on-device passive
acoustic species identification. NCBI for taxonomy.

The intelligence layer is *already public*. The work of PLATS-
Bioacoustic is to braid these threads into a single Relic
schema. The Relic is what the working artist holds. Every Relic
carries the full intelligence chain.

### Layer 4 — Sonification

This is where the field's most original contribution lives. The
EcoScore.

An EcoScore is a *named, reusable mapping* from ecological
variable to audible parameter. Crucibla and Resonate read
EcoScores as control signals. The first registry:

- `gbif_density_to_filter` — GBIF occurrence density across a
  polygon becomes a low-pass cutoff envelope. Where there is
  more life, the filter opens. Where there is silence in the
  data, the filter closes.
- `acoustic_complexity_to_tempo` — ACI index becomes BPM. The
  rhythm of the cut follows the acoustic complexity of the
  reference field recording.
- `iucn_status_to_dissonance` — Least Concern to Critically
  Endangered becomes a harmonic ratio from consonant to tritone.
  Extinction enters the music as audible tension.
- `range_centroid_to_pan` — the lat/lng centroid of a species's
  range becomes stereo pan + reverb tail. The geography speaks
  through space and depth.
- `strangeness_to_modulation_depth` — the Vivarium strangeness
  index becomes FM modulation depth. The queerer the phenomenon,
  the wider the modulation.

These mappings are not arbitrary. They are *codifiable
translations*. They are run inside **Max/MSP** patches that the
writer can compose into Crucibla projects. They are also run
inside Resonate as live-modulation control signals — a passive
AudioMoth feed mid-session becomes a slow harmonic shift across
the work being performed.

### Layer 5 — Visualisation

The visualisation layer is where PLATS-Bioacoustic enters the
visual-art tradition without losing the ecological signal.

Three backends, named:

**Pyo + matplotlib** runs server-side at Relic ingest. Every
ecological recording renders a spectrogram + waterfall + FFT GIF.
These are cached in storage and surface on the Relic detail page.
They are the field's *standard visual notation* — what a
spectrogram is to ecoacoustics.

**Houdini** is the next layer up. Each Relic emits a `.hip`
template pre-wired with the audio + spectrogram + GIS layer.
Houdini's **Geometry Nodes / SOPs / VOPs** read the recording as
geometry. Particle systems are seeded from species-occurrence
density. Niagara emitters in Houdini render a coral reef as a
forest of acoustic-shrimp clicks visualised as point clouds.
Volcanic tremor becomes a slow displacement of a mountain mesh.
Pulsar timing becomes a strict cosmological tick that drives a
fractal subdivision.

The argument here is that Houdini is the *industry-standard
procedural geometry environment* and ecological signals are
*procedural data*. The marriage is technical, not aesthetic-
analogical.

**TouchDesigner** runs the live-performance surface. A `.toe`
template per Relic with the audio + spectrogram + GIS layer
pre-wired. The TouchDesigner MCP bridge in the writer's existing
workspace makes this composable today.

Unreal sits at Layer 5b for spatial computing applications:
LiDAR point clouds load as Niagara emitters; species ranges drive
particle behaviour; MetaHumans are re-coloured by the Sphere's
mythic posture. The frontier here is the *spatial-computing
content market*, a category in which provenance is rapidly
becoming a regulated requirement.

### Layer 6 — Mythic posture

The required field. Every Relic carries a mythic posture — a
sentence the writer wrote that names the *angle of approach*.
"The elephant is grieving the mine, not the season." "The reef
chorus is the world's funeral mass." "The pulsar is the cosmos
breathing through gritted teeth."

This is the layer the field must not skip. Without mythic
posture the work is taxidermy. With it, the work is in dialogue
with a tradition that runs from Aby Warburg's *Mnemosyne Atlas*
through Donna Haraway through Robin Wall Kimmerer through
Octavia Butler.

The mythic posture is the *honesty* of the instrument. A
spectrogram without mythic posture is data. A spectrogram with
mythic posture is testimony.

## IV.5 — The Tesla parallel, named

The parallel is exact and worth making plain.

Nikola Tesla did not invent electricity. The electromagnetic
spectrum had always been there. Faraday had measured it; Maxwell
had described it mathematically. What Tesla did was *build the
instruments that made the invisible spectrum workable as a
discipline*. The alternating-current motor, the rotating
magnetic field, the Tesla coil, the proposal for wireless power
transmission. Each artefact moved a previously-invisible field
into the working hands of working engineers.

PLATS-Bioacoustic is the same move at a different layer.

The bioacoustic, substrate, eDNA, microbial, pulsar, and
mythological-cultural spectra have always been there. The
researchers cited above measured them. The lineage authors
described them. What PLATS-Bioacoustic does is *build the
instruments that make these spectra workable as a discipline for
the working artist*. The Relic, the EcoScore, the Cascade, the
saro-index, the Diepiri-balanced canon. Each artefact moves a
previously-invisible field into the working hands of working
artists.

The structural argument is identical. The political stakes are
larger.

Tesla's frontier was electrification — a century-long project
that reorganised every working life on the planet. The frontier
electrification touched was already visible to capital and
nation-states; Tesla extended what was already a recognised
problem.

PLATS-Bioacoustic's frontier is *provenance and biodiversity in
the AI cycle* — a multi-decadal project that will reorganise
every cultural-production workflow on the planet. The frontier
provenance touches is *not yet visible to capital and nation-
states as the same problem*. The discipline anticipates a
regulatory and ecological convergence that has not occurred yet.

The act of naming a field before it is recognised is the move
Tesla made and the move PLATS-Bioacoustic makes. The reward
arrives when the field is recognised. The obligation runs in the
present tense.

Tesla wrote: *Our virtues and our failings are inseparable, like
force and matter. When they separate, man is no more.* The
ecological reading is direct. The bioacoustic reading is direct.
The political reading is direct.

PLATS-Bioacoustic is the discipline that refuses the separation.

## V. The political weight

PLATS-Bioacoustic carries an `saro_index` on every Relic. The
index is named for Ken Saro-Wiwa, the Ogoni writer-activist
hanged by the Abacha regime in 1995 for organising the Movement
for the Survival of the Ogoni People against Shell's destruction
of the Niger Delta.

The index runs from zero to one. A pristine glacier lake nobody
is destroying scores zero point one. A mangrove kill site at
Bonny scores one point zero.

This is not symbolic. The data structure refuses ecological
wonder without political witness. When you query the Relic
catalogue for Northwestern North American reef recordings, you
also see the saro-index pip. You cannot work with the recording
without seeing the score. You can choose to work with it anyway.
The choice is yours, made deliberately, on the record.

The discipline holds wonder and witness in the same frame. This
is the move that distinguishes PLATS-Bioacoustic from charismatic-
megafauna ecotourism rhetoric. Without the political weight, the
work is extraction with a soundtrack. With it, the work is what
Saidiya Hartman called *witnessing-as-method for the
unwitnessable*.

The named lineage carries this argument and must be cited
together:

- **Donna Haraway** (*Staying with the Trouble*, *Companion Species
  Manifesto*) — tentacular thinking, sympoiesis, the Chthulucene.
- **Anna Tsing** (*The Mushroom at the End of the World*) —
  pericapitalist assemblages, contamination as collaboration.
- **Stacy Alaimo** (*Bodily Natures*) — trans-corporeality.
- **Karen Barad** (*Meeting the Universe Halfway*) — agential
  realism, diffraction, queer atoms.
- **Catriona Sandilands** (*Queer Ecologies*, with Bruce
  Erickson) — the naming text.
- **Robin Wall Kimmerer** (*Braiding Sweetgrass*) — Potawatomi
  botanist, reciprocity as method.
- **Kyle Powys Whyte** — Indigenous environmental ethics,
  Anishinaabe.
- **Ken Saro-Wiwa** (*A Month and a Day*) — Ogoni, hanged 1995.
  The `saro_index` is named for him.
- **Edith Widder** — deep-sea bioluminescence, the eternal

- **Octavia Butler** (*Parable*, *Xenogenesis*) — speculative
  grounding.
- **N.K. Jemisin** (*Broken Earth* trilogy) — geology as agent.
- **Ursula K. Le Guin** (*Always Coming Home*) — slow utopia

This list is what PLATS-Bioacoustic stands with. The discipline
cites accurately or it is not honest.

## VI. Diepiri-balanced canon

The default in every reference tool currently available — Pinterest,
Are.na, Cosmos, Eagle, even most art-history databases — ranks
significance by Eurocentric inheritance. The default is not
neutral. The default is the colonial gaze converted to a database
schema.

PLATS-Bioacoustic refuses this at the architecture layer.

The Diepiri-balanced canon is a structural rule: every reference
panel weights five axes equally on the default prior — Africa,
Asia, the West, Ancient, Contemporary. Personal lineage drift is
allowed: every accept-reject by the writer shifts the personal
weights by two-tenths of a percent. The default order resets to
balanced with one click.

The rule applies to:

- Cultural-reference suggestions in the Source Cascade.
- Archetypal canon panels in the Archetypal Lineage system.
- Mythological-layer suggestions on Locations.
- Theme cultural-ancestor balance pips.
- Sphere Stela suggestions.
- Provenance-receipt citation order.

The Diepiri rule is named after the writer's own middle name. It
inherits the writer's lineage as the structural argument. The
discipline cannot be applied without the rule because the rule
is *in the schema*. The default surfaces the world in proportion.
The writer chooses what to learn from. The world's geography
stops being a colonial inheritance and becomes a question.

This is the move other reference tools cannot make because their
training corpora cannot be retroactively rebalanced. PLATS-
Bioacoustic makes the move because its corpus is curated, on the
record, and corrected continuously against the default.

## VII. The frontier — what is now possible

This section is where the article must hold the trillion-dollar
arguments without losing the obligation.

### The AI provenance crisis

OpenAI, Stability, and every diffusion-model house is currently
exposed to billions in legal liability for opaque training data.
Multiple major suits are in process. The market for *verified
provenance* in AI-mediated cultural production is going to
materialise inside this decade. Estimates from McKinsey and
similar place the global AI provenance market between fifty
billion and three hundred billion dollars by 2032.

PLATS-Bioacoustic, through its Lukasa LRC layer, *already
produces the receipts*. Every Relic that touches a generative
pipeline carries its full ai_transforms chain. Every model that
contributed leaves a receipt. The discipline is structurally
ready for a regulatory environment that will require what the
discipline already does.

The frontier is not market opportunity. The frontier is the
*obligation* to make every cultural output verifiable. The
trillion-dollar number is what the market will spend trying to
catch up to what PLATS-Bioacoustic does as a matter of course.

### The biodiversity crisis as cultural ground

The Convention on Biological Diversity has named a global
biodiversity funding gap of seven hundred billion dollars a year.
Carbon markets are scaling. Biodiversity credits are emerging.
The cultural-content layer for these markets — the
*soundscape*, the *visualisation*, the *story* — is what makes
the markets legible to the public who pays.

PLATS-Bioacoustic produces *sonified evidence* of biodiversity
state. A coral reef chorus rendered as audio with provenance
receipts is *climate testimony in cultural form*. As biodiversity
markets scale, the demand for accompanying cultural artifacts —
films, sound installations, museum content, immersive
experiences, journalistic accompaniment — scales with them. The
addressable market is *climate-adjacent cultural production at
the scale climate spending will reach*.

This is not a venture proposition. This is a *cultural
obligation that pays*.

### The wellness and sleep economy

The global wellness market is six trillion dollars. Sleep
specifically is five hundred billion. Sound-based wellness is
the fastest-growing sub-segment. Calm, Headspace, Endel, and
the brown-noise YouTube channels are already at the front of
this. They use sonified natural systems without provenance.

PLATS-Bioacoustic provides *provenanced* sonified natural
systems. The infrasonic elephant rumble that lowers your
heart rate is *audibly the actual elephant*, recorded in a
named research project, cited in your wellness app. The
sonified reef chorus that helps you sleep is *the same reef
that scored 0.7 on the saro-index because it is in active
bleaching*. The user listens with knowledge. The wellness
becomes witness.

The category is *witnessing wellness*. It does not yet exist.
It will.

### Spatial computing and Unreal worlds

The spatial-computing content market — Vision Pro, Quest, the
upcoming generation of headsets — is projected at one trillion
dollars by 2032. Every piece of content in this market needs
real ecological provenance to land as more than decoration.

PLATS-Bioacoustic *emits Unreal assets* directly. A Relic
includes an Unreal asset path and Blueprint when applicable.
The point clouds, the species-range Niagara emitters, the
mythic-posture-coloured MetaHumans — these are *first-class
exports*, not afterthoughts.

A spatial-computing application built on PLATS-Bioacoustic data
carries its citations. The viewer in their headset can read who
recorded the elephant rumble that scores the room they stand
in. The discipline scales to spatial computing without losing
its receipts.

### Drug discovery and natural products

Biodiversity-derived drug discovery is a forty-billion-dollar
sector annually. Compound discovery depends on field collection
of biological material. Field collection depends on knowing
where to look. eDNA inventory — already in the PLATS-Bioacoustic
intelligence layer — accelerates compound discovery by orders of
magnitude.

A Relic with kind=species and a documented eDNA trace from a
remote ecosystem is *pharmaceutical reconnaissance data with
artistic provenance*. The discipline as scientific instrument
intersects with the discipline as cultural instrument here.

### Decolonial premium

Brand premium for verified-decolonial cultural production is
measurable and rising. Nike, Patagonia, Adidas, every luxury
house in Europe is paying for it. Most pay for *appearance* of
decolonial work. Few can pay for *structural* decolonial work
because no structural decolonial tool exists.

The Diepiri-balanced canon is the structural decolonial tool.
The `saro_index` is the political audit. The named lineage is
the citation. PLATS-Bioacoustic does not perform decolonial work.
It *enacts* it at the schema level.

This is what makes the discipline durable in a market that will

## VIII. Where the article situates itself in art history

PLATS-Bioacoustic does not invent. It composes a tradition.

**Aby Warburg's *Mnemosyne Atlas*** (1924-1929) was an attempt to
build the cultural geography of European image-traditions as a
relational atlas. The Atlas was unfinished at his death. The
project remains foundational to art history.

PLATS-Bioacoustic is *Warburg's Atlas applied to the ecological
world*, with the colonial defaults removed and the political
witness installed at the schema layer. The Themes architecture
is the Atlas's *bilderfahrzeuge* concept made operational. The
Locations architecture extends the Atlas into specific places
with mythological accretion. The Source Cascade is the Atlas's
citation chain made executable.

**Bernie Krause's** *The Great Animal Orchestra* (2012) is the
field's canonical popular text. Krause coined the terms biophony,
geophony, anthrophony. PLATS-Bioacoustic extends his framework
with the cultural-political layer he could not (in his role as
field recordist) carry alone.

**Edith Widder's** deep-sea bioluminescence work — the eternal
strangeness — is the discipline's spiritual ground floor. There
are seventy-five percent more known animal species in the deep
ocean than on land. Most of them produce light. We can listen and
we can record. We have barely begun.

**Donna Haraway's** *Staying with the Trouble* names the
posture. Haraway insists that the move available to us in this
century is not solution but composition. PLATS-Bioacoustic
composes ecological data with mythic posture with political
witness with verified provenance.

This is the lineage. The article cites it accurately.
