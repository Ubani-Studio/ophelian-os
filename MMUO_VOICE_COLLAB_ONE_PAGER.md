# Voice collaboration, plain English

> Hand this to any artist friend you want to invite as a voice
> contributor. Plain language version of the Voice Royalty Protocol.
> One A4 page printable. Editable as needed per friend.

---

## What we're making

A character world called **Boveda**. Each character is voiced by a real
person rather than synthetic AI defaults. The voices are owned by the
artists who lend them, with technical safeguards (provenance,
revocation, royalty splits) and a settlement layer that tracks every
use.

You'd be voicing one or more characters in this world.

## What I'm asking from you

A short studio session, **30 to 90 minutes** of recording, varied
delivery (declarative, intimate, performing, asking, laughing). You
read passages I provide. You can edit / refuse any line.

The audio gets used to train a voice clone of you, owned by Mmuo
(my proprietary voice synthesis system, hosted on my servers). Once
trained, the system can generate new lines in your voice for the
character you're voicing, in scenes I write.

## What you keep

- **Your voice is yours.** The clone is keyed to your real identity.
  You can revoke its use at any time.
- **Provenance is permanent.** Every line ever generated logs against
  your voice id. You can audit usage at any point.
- **You see every use case** if you want. Scenes, scripts, public
  releases, all queryable.
- **You can say no** to specific use cases (commercial, brand, NSFW,
  political, anything that doesn't fit your values).

## License options, pick one or hybrid

### Tier A, "this character, this universe"

- Use your voice for the agreed character only.
- All Boveda-internal use OK (writing, scenes, prototyping, releases
  tied to this universe).
- Commercial use OK, royalty share applies (see below).
- Revocation right: you can pull your voice from any future renders
  with 30 days notice. Already-rendered audio stays as it is.

### Tier B, "non-commercial only"

- Same as Tier A but commercial uses (paid placements, brand work,
  ad campaigns) require a separate one-off conversation each time.
- Better fit if you want to participate in the artistic project but
  not the business side.

### Tier C, "co-ownership with on-chain royalty"

- You become a co-owner of revenue derived from any work using your
  voice on the agreed character.
- Default split: **50% to you, 50% to me**, settled automatically
  on-chain (Polygon, USDC) via the Imperium settlement layer.
  Adjustable by mutual agreement.
- You don't have to manage anything; payments arrive automatically
  when revenue events fire.

You can pick different tiers for different characters or different
contexts. Hybrid is fine.

## What I keep

- Right to render new lines for the agreed character in the agreed
  contexts.
- The trained voice model files (you don't need to host or maintain
  them; they live on my Modal infrastructure).
- Right to use the system to render lines in service of the project
  we agreed to.

I do **not** keep:

- Right to use your voice for any character or context outside what
  we explicitly agreed.
- Right to license your voice to third parties without your consent.
- Right to keep using your voice after you revoke.

## Recording session expectations

Studio: my treated home studio, or a pro studio if needed. Mic:
Neumann U87 + Avedis MA5 (top-shelf pro signal chain).

I'll send you the passages a few days before. They'll come from the
character's existing writing voice (think 30 lines of varied delivery,
nothing weird). You can edit, swap, or rewrite anything that doesn't
feel like you.

We record together if local, or you record at your end with a
provided remote setup if not.

## After the session

1. Audio gets cleaned (silence trimming, no compression, no fancy
   processing). Raw and cleaned versions both kept.
2. Provenance gets registered: timestamp, audio fingerprint, license
   terms, your real identity, all signed and stored.
3. Voice clone trains overnight on Modal (~$2 in GPU compute, my cost).
4. We listen to a few generated lines together. You approve or we
   iterate.
5. The character starts speaking in your voice in Boveda from then
   on. Royalty tracking is automatic.

## Revocation

You email or text me. I disable rendering using your voice within 24
hours. Already-rendered audio stays in whatever it's in (you can
request specific takedowns separately, handled case by case).

## Compensation

For Tier A and Tier B, no upfront cash. Compensation is creative
co-credit ("voice: <your name>") plus per-use royalties at the
agreed rate.

For Tier C, royalty splits as agreed, settled on-chain. Typical 50/50.

If you want **flat-fee work** (e.g. you want to be paid per session,
not based on royalties), we can structure that separately. Common
indie rate: **£250 to £500 for a 90-minute session** with no further
rights. Negotiable.

## What you should do before agreeing

- Listen to the existing Boveda character's voice (writing samples
  attached). Decide if you want to live in their cadence.
- Read this whole page. Ask anything.
- Talk to me about specifics: which character, which tier, anything
  you want to lock down.
